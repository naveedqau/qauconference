About The Conference

Honoring a Visionary Leader at Quaid-i-Azam University

Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU), Pakistan's premier institution of higher learning, proudly hosts a flagship 3-day International Academic Conference celebrating the 150th Birth Anniversary of the Founder of the Nation, Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

This global symposium brings together world-renowned historians, political scientists, legal scholars, diplomats, and young researchers to re-examine Jinnah's constitutionalism, democratic foresight, minority rights framework, and economic vision for South Asia in the 21st century.

150+ Research Papers
25+ International Scholars

Call for Research Papers

We invite original research contributions under the following sub-themes:

  • Constitutionalism & Rule of Law: Jinnah's Legal Philosophy
  • Foreign Policy & Peace: Regional Stability in South Asia
  • Education & Youth Empowerment: QAU Memorial Lectures
  • Minority Rights & Inclusion: The August 11, 1947 Vision
Abstract Deadline: October 15, 2026
Full Paper Submission: November 15, 2026
Acceptance Notification: December 01, 2026
