About The Sesquicentennial Conference

Preserving Legacy, Guiding Modern Statehood at QAU

Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU), Pakistan's premier seat of higher learning, proudly convenes an international academic conference celebrating the 150th Birth Anniversary of the Founder of the Nation, Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

This global symposium invites eminent historians, constitutional experts, political analysts, and researchers to re-examine Jinnah's vision for democratic governance, institutional integrity, human rights, and peaceful international diplomacy in South Asia.

Academic Call

Call for Research Papers

We invite original research paper submissions under the following official sub-themes:

  • Constitutionalism & Rule of Law: Jinnah's Legal Philosophy & Jurisprudence
  • Foreign Policy & Peace: Diplomatic Foresight for Regional Stability
  • Education & Youth Empowerment: Memorial Lectures & Educational Reform
  • Minority Rights & Inclusion: The Historic August 11, 1947 Framework
Abstract Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026
Full Paper Submission: November 15, 2026
Acceptance Notification: December 01, 2026
